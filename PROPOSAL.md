# Project Proposal: Equity Performance Gap Analysis

Title: **Explaining the Performance Gap between U.S. and European Equities through Macroeconomic and Structural Factors**:

This project aims to analyze and model the differences in stock market returns between the United States and Europe using linear and polynomial regression methods. The goal is to evaluate how measurable macroeconomic factors may explain the historical outperformance of U.S. equities compared to European ones.

The analysis will rely on real, publicly available datasets. The main explanatory factors include the sectoral composition of the indices (with particular attention to the technology sector), the EUR/USD exchange rate, and monetary policy indicators such as the Federal Reserve and European Central Bank policy rates. A measure of volatility will also be incorporated to capture market risk.

The project will apply several key concepts covered in the course, including linear and polynomial regression, gradient descent, and the bias-variance tradeoff. These theoretical ideas will be implemented using standard data science tools: pandas for data manipulation, seaborn and matplotlib for data visualization, and scikit-learn for model fitting and validation.

The results will provide an empirical comparison of how these macroeconomic variables influence the returns of the S&P 500 and the Euro Stoxx 50, complemented by a qualitative discussion of structural factors such as innovation, venture capital, and corporate culture that cannot be directly quantified.
